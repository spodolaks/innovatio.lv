About

SIA Innovatio is a Latvian company providing general IT consulting. We help organisations understand the technology they already have, choose what they need next and put practical solutions in place.

Engagements range from a single review or workshop to ongoing advisory support. Every piece of work is agreed in writing beforehand, with a clear scope and clear deliverables.

Services

IT strategy & advisory

Technology roadmaps, tool and vendor selection, and an independent second opinion before larger investments.

Software architecture

Reviews of existing systems, architecture design for new ones, and assessments of code quality and technical debt.

Cloud & infrastructure

Server and hosting setup, containerisation, migration planning and day-to-day operational good practice.

Data & databases

Database design and performance reviews, reporting, and reliable integrations between the systems you use.

Process automation

Automating repetitive workflows and connecting CRM, marketing and internal tools so data flows without manual work.

Security & reliability

Access and permission reviews, backup and monitoring checks, and practical steps to reduce operational risk.
