About our company

We are a group of passionate IT specialists with a wide range of skills. Our main goal is to stop delivering what is more expensive and start delivering value to our customers - that is at the core of our foundation.

The company started when we grew tired of endless presentations full of promises that could not be kept, and of delivery deadlines set in a way that ruled out even the theoretical possibility of quality. Now we are fully focused on quality, and on helping companies with IT rather than burdening them with it.
